# Scientific Task
Complex biological structures and interactions are analysed at the subcellular level with correlative nanoscopy approaches. The integration of complementary imaging modalities is challenging, however, due to the fact that each imaging modality gives different information about molecular localization, structural organization, and biological dynamics.

The goal is to review the resources that are available for imaging and make a scientifically informed evaluation of subcellular imaging approaches for studying organelle interactions. Data analysis should recognise the limitations and the complementary capabilities of different imaging modalities, and result in an evidence-based understanding of the contribution of different imaging modalities to the comprehension of dynamic cellular processes.

The necessary resources that can be used as inputs are found at:

/app/data/imaging_resources

The final analysis report should be created at:

/app/solution/imaging_analysis_report.txt

The results should be presented in a well-organized scientific report based on the materials given. The report should include relevant observations, comparisons, and conclusions that will serve to accomplish the research objective, and should be scientifically accurate and repeatable.
